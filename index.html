1. Information We Collect

Touch Blocker does not collect any personal information directly. However, our app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, which may collect certain data as described below.

2. Third-Party Services

We use Google AdMob to serve ads. AdMob may collect and use data including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Usage data for ad personalization

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Permissions Used

Our app requests the following permissions:

  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Required to display the floating lock bubble over other apps.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Required to keep the lock service running while you use other apps.
  • INTERNET: Required to load advertisements.
  • VIBRATE: Used for haptic feedback when locking/unlocking.

4. Data Retention

Touch Blocker does not store any personal data on our servers. Any data collected by AdMob is subject to Google's data retention policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
